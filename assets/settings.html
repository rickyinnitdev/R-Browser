Settings

Minimal by design. Nothing leaves this machine unless you load a page.

Ad & tracker blocking
Network-level filtering with the bundled EasyList / EasyPrivacy rule set. Requests are answered locally — nothing is proxied.
Cosmetic filtering
Hides leftover ad slots, sponsored modules and sticky banners so the page looks clean, not just broken.
Anti-adblock defence
Neutralises bait detection, stubs common detector libraries and removes "please disable your ad blocker" walls.
Remove cookie banners
Also hides consent walls and newsletter interstitials.
Encrypted DNS & Wi-Fi protection
Encrypts DNS lookups via HTTPS (DoH) and enables Encrypted Client Hello (ECH). Blinds your Wi-Fi router, admin and ISP to what websites you visit. Needs a restart.
Sleep background tabs
Sleeps idle tabs after about 45 seconds. Tabs playing audio stay awake; restored tabs load when opened.
Restore last session
Reopens your tabs next time R Browser starts.
Hardware acceleration
Smoother scrolling and video, but the graphics process costs roughly 100 MB. Off keeps R Browser lean. Needs a restart.
Download acceleration (multi-stream)
Bypasses server speed limits on file hosts by opening parallel range streams directly to disk.
Vertical tabs (sidebar)
Display tabs in a vertical sidebar on the left instead of horizontal tabs across the top.
Side panel on left
Dock the Bookmarks, History and Shields side panel on the left side of the window.
